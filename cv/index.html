CV

Andreas Gajdošík is a Czech new media artist working with useful art and socially engaged projects through programming, interventions, and performance. His interests include digital sovereignty, decentralized media, ethical/open source software, sustainability, and veganism. He is a member of automatonomy.net and teaches at the Faculty of Fine Arts, Brno University of Technology. He received the Jindřich Chalupecký Award in 2019.
