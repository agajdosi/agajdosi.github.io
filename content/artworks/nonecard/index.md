NoneCard (2025) is an internet art application for sharing customers cards.
An artist, his friends and random people on the internet are sharing and using the customer cards to get their discounts while keeping the data obfuscated.
As result of this _identity mixing_ a shared data identity is created in the databases of the shops and corporations.
And a digital subject which is completely misleading and worthless is created.
The application is available at https://nonecard.gajdosik.org.
